What is e-waste?

When tech reaches the end of its life, it becomes e-waste.

The short answer

E-waste means electronic products and electrical parts that we no longer use, need or want.

Think about the things around you

  • Old phones
  • Broken laptops
  • Headphones that stopped working
  • Keyboards
  • Chargers and cables
  • Printers
  • Batteries
  • Old game controllers

The cool part

E-waste is not just waste. Inside old electronics are tiny components, useful materials and clever technology that can be repaired, reused, recovered or recycled.

Metals

Copper, aluminium, iron and gold.

Components

Motors, LEDs, switches and speakers.

Parts

Wires, circuit boards, connectors and magnets.

E-waste and the law in India

India has rules for this: the E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022. They make the companies that make and sell electronics responsible for getting old products collected and recycled. This idea is called Extended Producer Responsibility, or EPR.

Recyclers have to be registered and authorised. That is why it matters who you hand your old devices to.
